Roxas by Yoko Shimomura from Kingdom Hearts II

This is the first arrangement I completed. I referenced a piano arrangement by Sachiko Miyano from the book "ピアノコレクションズ キングダムハーツ (Piano Collections Kingdom Hearts)". For the most part I am happy with the result. I'm still unsure about the second half when the piece goes down an octave. At that point I started thinking the piece should probably be played in drop D but I didn't want to go back and change the rest of the arrangement so some of the chords ended up sounding incomplete.
